How do I play?

Tap a tile to turn its track. When the route is ready, press Depart. Collect the postcards on the way and bring the train to the station.

What are tickets?

A ticket is used only when a run fails. You can hold up to 5 tickets, and one refills every 30 minutes. You can also trade 3 bolts for a ticket.

What are moves and bolts?

Each stop allows a number of tile turns (moves). Bolts are earned on the track and by finishing in few moves; 3 bolts give you +5 moves.

I bought something but don't see it

Open the Market and tap “Restore purchases”, signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy. Purchases are handled by Apple; for refunds visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress; purchases can always be restored.
