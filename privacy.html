Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

In short: Grandpa's Train has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and we do not collect your personal data. Two things leave your device: the purchase information Apple and the in-app purchase system need to process a purchase, and, from version 1.0.1, anonymous gameplay statistics that help us balance the game (see below).

Data we collect

We, the developer, do not collect data about you. The game does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (stops reached, postcards, tickets, moves, bolts, and settings such as sound and language) is saved only on your device. It is never uploaded. Deleting the app removes it.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details. Apple's handling of your data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

The game uses Unity In-App Purchasing (Unity Technologies) to talk to the App Store. When a purchase or restore runs, it sends Unity the purchase and transaction details (product, price, transaction ID), app and device identifiers (such as an installation or vendor identifier), technical information (device model, OS version, language, locale) and your approximate country derived from your IP address. Unity uses this to process and verify purchases, prevent duplicate deliveries and report revenue; it is not used for advertising or tracking. See Unity's Privacy Policy.

Anonymous gameplay statistics

From version 1.0.1 the game sends anonymous usage events to TelemetryDeck (TelemetryDeck GmbH, Germany), a privacy-focused analytics service. They tell us things like how many players start or finish a stop, how many moves a run took, where players get stuck, which offers are shown and whether a purchase went through.

Each event carries the event name and its details, the app version, the device model, the OS version and the game language. Instead of any device or advertising identifier, the game creates a random value on first launch and sends only a hash of it; TelemetryDeck hashes it again before storing. No name, email, location, contacts or advertising identifier is ever sent, and the data is not used for advertising or tracking and not combined with data from other apps. See TelemetryDeck's Privacy Policy.

Advertising and tracking

The game shows no ads, contains no advertising SDK, does not use your advertising identifier and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Grandpa's Train is suitable for all ages. Apart from the purchase information and the anonymous statistics described above, the game sends no data from anyone, including children, and it never asks for personal information.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
