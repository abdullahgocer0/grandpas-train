Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

In short: Grandpa's Train has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and we do not collect your personal data. The only data that leaves your device is the purchase information Apple and the in-app purchase system need to process and verify a purchase (see below).

Data we collect

We, the developer, do not collect data about you. The game does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (stops reached, postcards, tickets, moves, bolts, and settings such as sound and language) is saved only on your device. It is never uploaded. Deleting the app removes it.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details. Apple's handling of your data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

The game uses Unity In-App Purchasing (Unity Technologies) to talk to the App Store. When a purchase or restore runs, it sends Unity the purchase and transaction details (product, price, transaction ID), app and device identifiers (such as an installation or vendor identifier), technical information (device model, OS version, language, locale) and your approximate country derived from your IP address. Unity uses this to process and verify purchases, prevent duplicate deliveries and report revenue; it is not used for advertising or tracking. See Unity's Privacy Policy.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

The game shows no ads, contains no advertising SDK, does not use your advertising identifier and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Grandpa's Train is suitable for all ages. Apart from the purchase information described above, which is sent only when someone buys or restores a purchase, the game sends no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
