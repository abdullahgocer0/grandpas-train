Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

In short: Grandpa's Train does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

Data we collect

None. The game does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information, and it does not send data about you or your device to us or to anyone else.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (stops reached, postcards, tickets, moves, bolts, and settings such as sound and language) is saved only on your device. It is never uploaded. Deleting the app removes it.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details. The game only checks, on your device, which items you own so it can unlock them and restore them when you tap “Restore purchases”. Apple's handling of your data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

The game shows no ads, contains no analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Grandpa's Train is suitable for all ages. Because the game collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
